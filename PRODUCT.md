# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers and graduate students of the lab who are starting an empirical paper, mostly in computer science and
AI. They have read some of the literature, have an idea, and need to turn it into experiments that a reviewer
will accept and that someone else can run.

## Product Purpose

Research Design takes a writing project from papers to a frozen experiment plan. It helps the researcher compare
what earlier papers measured and how, state questions and hypotheses, choose a design and face its threats,
define each metric, pre-specify the analysis, and lay out the runs. It succeeds when the plan can be executed in
Scientific Research Desktop without a question back to its author, and when every later result can be traced to
the hypothesis and metric it was planned for.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear and trustworthy. Calm and exact; the researcher decides, the tool keeps the record.

## Anti-references

- A chat box that writes a plan nobody checked.
- Leaderboards and "state of the art" numbers detached from their papers.
- Dense decoration, gradients, marketing-style hero sections inside task screens.
- Forms that ask for everything before showing anything useful.

## Design Principles

1. Show the chain. Question, hypothesis, variable, metric, analysis and runs are visibly linked, in both directions.
2. Keep every extracted fact next to the sentence it came from.
3. Separate what a paper reported, what the AI suggested and what the researcher decided.
4. Say what a design cannot support before the experiment is run.
5. A frozen plan stays frozen; a change is a new version that says why.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility and semantic controls. Never use colour as
the only status signal. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Vietnamese interface copy; English source text and
plan content are shown exactly as written.
